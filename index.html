Introduction

Welcome! This site is dedicated to understanding and combating the increasing threat of wildfires, particularly in Bukidnon. Wildfires not only destroy forests and habitats but also contribute significantly to climate change.

Bukidnon, known for its lush landscapes, is increasingly vulnerable. Understanding the causes and implementing preventative measures are crucial.

Climate Action and Wildfires

Climate change exacerbates wildfire risks through increased temperatures and prolonged droughts. Learn how climate action can mitigate these risks.

  • Reducing carbon emissions
  • Promoting sustainable land management
  • Supporting reforestation efforts
