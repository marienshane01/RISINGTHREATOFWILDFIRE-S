Climate Action: Wildfire Monitoring

Wildfire monitoring is a critical part of climate action, especially as rising temperatures and prolonged droughts increase the risk of forest fires. Monitoring involves tracking weather conditions, forest dryness, temperature levels, wind patterns, and human activities that may trigger fires. Early detection through satellites, drones, and ground sensors helps authorities respond quickly before fires spread uncontrollably.

By monitoring wildfire-prone areas, communities can reduce damage to forests, wildlife, homes, and human lives. Accurate data allows governments and environmental agencies to plan prevention strategies, issue early warnings, and evaluate how climate change contributes to the increasing intensity and frequency of wildfires. Monitoring helps protect ecosystems today while securing a safer and more resilient future.
